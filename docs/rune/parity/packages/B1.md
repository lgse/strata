## What dependents must know

- The contract is the module documentation of `crates/strata-app/src/slots.rs`; read it
  whole, "Who owns what" and "Saving a setting" included. `columns/part.rs` is the worked
  example and `docs/workspace.md` points to it.
- A part is a module and two lines in `lib.rs` under its package's comment: `mod <module>;`
  and `<field>: <module>::<State>,` inside `slots::parts!`. The package that fills a slot
  deletes the one `expect(dead_code)` line on that slot's `Place` variant, and edits
  nothing else in `slots.rs`.
- `slots.rs`, `shell.rs`, `slots/`, `host/`, `test_support.rs`, `events.rs`, `listing.rs`
  and `columns/` belong to the contract: a change there is a `contract` pull request of its
  own.
- A view's place is `Place::View(View { build, shows, focus, pointer_at, rows, editing,
  probe })`; a sidebar's is `Place::Sidebar(Sidebar { build, focus })`; an overlay or a
  dialog pushes its surfaces on `Frame::layer`. `test_support::hosted_over` tests a place
  without registering it. The notes left for [A1](A1.md), [A2](A2.md), [A3](A3.md),
  [A4](A4.md), [A8](A8.md) and [H3](H3.md) are in their files.
- A part keeps no `Rc<Browser>` of its own, reads `app.browser` at the call and can build
  everything it shows again from the browser, so that tabs ([A4](A4.md)) and a live view
  switch ([A8](A8.md)) stay open.
- Window chords and the header's five buttons were to wait on [B3](B3.md). `WINDOW_KEYS`
  and `shell::on_key` are gone since [#2210](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2210): the
  keys, the menu rows and the buttons are the command registry's
  (`crates/strata-app/src/commands.rs`), and [B3](B3.md), "What dependents must know", says
  what a part registers and when.
