## Record

Dates are GitHub's, in UTC.

- **2026-11-12, [#2181](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2181).** A `contract` change to the QA runner: the fuzz driver,
  `./scripts/qa-fuzz.sh`, in `tests/qa/fuzz.rs` and `tests/qa/fuzz/`. A seed and an
  alphabet's version write a sequence, a scenario file; a replay observes after every step,
  at rest (eight equal reads 50 ms apart); a comparator names the first differing field, a
  shrinker confirms three times out of three, and `normalisation.map` and
  `calibration.mask`, both empty, say what a comparison leaves out. It flips no run: F1 owns
  none.
- **The result: two GTK replays of one seed agree, with nothing masked, wherever GTK agrees
  with itself.** On the driver as it merges, with the `calibration` alphabet (Columns on the
  canonical fixture), 40 actions and two replays: 999 of 1,000 seeds at one sequence to a
  core, and 299 of 299 at 96 sequences on 24 cores. At 120 actions and three replays, 100 of
  100. No step was unsettled and no GLib warning logged in 139,867 observations. One step in
  ten is not at rest when its reply is written, and GTK goes on changing for up to 256 ms
  with nothing to see, 304 ms under that load, against a quiet window of 350 ms.
- **The calibration ran with `strata-next` at `682fa0e7f`** and was not repeated after #2192
  and #2195 to #2197 merged application code: the driver is identical, and the smoke tier
  agrees at the head.
- **Findings from the calibration:** five GTK faults, each an `oracle` finding
  ([findings](../findings.md)) with its sequence parked in
  `tests/qa/fuzz/corpus/calibration/`: [#2173](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2173), [#2176](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2176), [#2177](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2177), [#2180](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2180) and [#2201](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2201). The last was
  met by seed `0x1de` in the final-code run of seeds 1 to 1000, and parked after it.

## What dependents must know

- **[F2](F2.md), [F3](F3.md), [F4](F4.md) and every area package:** `docs/testing.md`, "Fuzzing", is the
  contract, and its last part, "What the next packages build on", is written for you: the
  sentence for the checkpoint, why "agree" is a rate (replay a GTK against Rune difference
  GTK against GTK 24 times before blaming Rune), where a replay starts its target
  (`Launch::binary`), what ends a replay, and why the `calibration` alphabet's rename guard
  is not for copying into the Columns pilot.
- A seed names a sequence only with the alphabet's version. Change an alphabet and its
  version and pin change with it (`tests/qa/fuzz/alphabets/pins`); a parked line and a kept
  file say their version.
- "Calibration" there says what the numbers do not cover: one alphabet and one view, delays
  longer than the 350 ms window, and a machine starved of CPU. An area measures its own.
- An area package's alphabet and smoke tier (area deliverable 6) can start now that
  [#2181](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2181) has merged.
