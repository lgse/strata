What the plan says of this package elsewhere, quoted with the section it is in:

- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** The fuzzer replays one generated input sequence on both targets and compares what a user could observe. It adds a generator, a comparator and a shrinker to the QA runner.
- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** Generate. A seed picks actions from an area's alphabet: keys, clicks and menu picks on accessibility targets, typed text, and runner-side `fs` changes. Actions name targets by role and name, never by coordinate, so each toolkit resolves them in its own geometry.
- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** Replay. The runner starts each target on a fresh copy of one fixture and sends the same steps.
- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** Observe at rest. Both targets settle after each step. The observation is a fixed projection of the reply's `app` snapshot, plus a digest of the fixture tree and of `settings.toml`.
- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** Shrink. Shorter sequences are replayed until the divergence is minimal, then confirmed three times out of three.
- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** Calibrate on GTK first. The slice's V3 found the `app` snapshot differs between two GTK runs of one scenario. The projection is fixed only once two GTK runs of a seed agree on it (F1).
- **Verification method, "Differential fuzzing".** Noise: The calibration mask grows, only when two GTK runs of the seed differ.
- **Conventions.** The header keys, the ledger fields and the file paths below are proposals for H1, H2 and F1 to settle; the rules are the point.
- **Conventions, "Fuzz artifacts".** Shrunk sequences live in a corpus folder per area, named by seed, and the smoke tier replays them. A sequence is written in scenario format, so a divergence replays with the ordinary runner. A sequence becomes a named scenario only when no scenario owns the behaviour, as the test-value rules in `AGENTS.md` ask.
- **Validation and gates, "Before a package's pull request".** The fuzz smoke tier for its alphabet: F1's command.

## What dependents must know

- Once [#2181](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2181) merges, `docs/testing.md`, "Fuzzing", is the contract. An area package's
  alphabet and smoke tier (area deliverable 6) come after that; do the rest first.
- A rule, a mask or a parked seed is one line, `<field> <code> <issue>`, sorted by field,
  and the issue is written `lgse/strata#<number>`: the driver refuses another form. What
  stands there for a finding recorded in [`findings.md`](../findings.md) is open
  ([`owner.md`](../owner.md#tracking-and-findings)).
- For [F2](F2.md): a replay starts the one target `STRATA_QA_TARGET` names for the whole
  process (`Launch::binary` in `tests/qa.rs`), so comparing GTK with Rune needs `Launch` to
  take the target, or two processes and `compare` over their kept streams. `--target rune`
  already builds `strata-rune` and replays on it.
- What the calibration does not cover is in the docs: one alphabet and one view,
  `app.view_mode` never moves, and a change after more than 350 ms of quiet is taken for the
  next step's.
