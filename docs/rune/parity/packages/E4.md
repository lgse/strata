## Record

- **2026-11-22 ([#2785](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2785)):** Retired the GTK application (`src/ui/`, `src/assets*`, `src/qa*`, `src/portal*`, `src/services/`, `src/util/`, `src/style.css`, `build.rs`, `tests/key_lookups*`), wired root `strata` directly to `strata_app::run`, moved `crates/strata-rune/tests/` (`window` and `qa_protocol`) to root `tests/` and removed `crates/strata-rune/`. Removed `gtk4`, `gdk4*`, `sourceview5`, and `gstreamer*` from `Cargo.toml`/`Cargo.lock` (keeping `gdk-pixbuf` only in `strata-preview` and `strata-fs` for sandboxed raster/thumbnail decoding) and banned them workspace-wide in `deny.toml`. Promoted all 7 Rune twin scenarios into the corpus (merging `camera-formats-preview-sandbox` across all 5 variants), retired GTK visual baselines, migrated all 37 `host = "session"` runs (14 scenarios) to native `"host": "session"` headers, promoted all 95 Rune mutation patches into `tests/qa/mutations/`, retired the parity ledger and differential fuzzing harness, and collapsed the 2-lane build (`scripts/lanes.py`) back to a single workspace. Of the 16 formerly `pending` runs, 11 now pass on Rune, 3 are marked `known_bug` at their exact failing steps, and 2 remain `skip` (fcitx5-chewing IME).

## Notes from other packages

From the owner's ruling of 2026-11-12 on thumbnails ([`owner.md`](../owner.md#ruled)): E4
owns removing `gdk-pixbuf` from `strata-rune`'s tree, and exit criterion 6 cannot pass until
it is gone. It is there already by two paths that have nothing to do with thumbnails in the
Rune interface: `strata-app` depends on `strata-fs`, and `crates/strata-fs/Cargo.toml`
depends on `gdk-pixbuf` directly (line 35) and on `strata-preview` (line 29), neither
optional. So E4's removal includes `strata-fs`'s own use of `gdk-pixbuf` and its use of
`strata-preview`; the `--browser-worker` entry point that `strata-rune` gains for thumbnail
workers adds a direct edge from `strata-app` to `strata-preview`, the third part. Whether E4 also removes it from `strata-fs`'s path, or criterion 6
is narrowed, is open for the owner ([`owner.md`](../owner.md#e4-exit-criterion-6)). It is an exception for `gdk-pixbuf` alone:
`gtk4`, `gdk4` and `sourceview5` stay out of `strata-app` and `strata-rune`, and
`cargo tree -p strata-rune -i gtk4` still finds nothing.
