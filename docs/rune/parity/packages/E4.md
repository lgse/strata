## Notes from other packages

From the owner's ruling of 2026-11-12 on thumbnails ([`owner.md`](../owner.md#ruled)): E4
owns removing `gdk-pixbuf` from `strata-rune`'s tree, and exit criterion 6 cannot pass until
it is gone. It is there already by two paths that have nothing to do with thumbnails in the
Rune interface: `strata-app` depends on `strata-fs`, and `crates/strata-fs/Cargo.toml`
depends on `gdk-pixbuf` directly (line 35) and on `strata-preview` (line 29), neither
optional. So E4's removal includes `strata-fs`'s own use of `gdk-pixbuf` and its use of
`strata-preview`; the `--browser-worker` entry point that `strata-rune` gains for thumbnail
workers adds a direct edge from `strata-app` to `strata-preview`, the third part. Whether E4 also removes it from `strata-fs`'s path, or criterion 6
is narrowed, is open for the owner ([`owner.md`](../owner.md#e4-exit-criterion-6)). It is an exception for `gdk-pixbuf` alone:
`gtk4`, `gdk4` and `sourceview5` stay out of `strata-app` and `strata-rune`, and
`cargo tree -p strata-rune -i gtk4` still finds nothing.

From the owner's instruction of 2026-11-12 that turned CI off for `strata-next`
([`owner.md`](../owner.md#ruled)): `ci.yml` on `strata-next` no longer runs for pushes to
`strata-next` or pull requests based on it. If `main` takes `strata-next` by merge at the
cutover, that trigger change must not reach `main` in a form that turns CI off for anything
`main` needs.
