## What dependents must know

- `pane.rs` is for [A2](A2.md): the List view stands on it as the Icons view does. It
  applies `events::Change` and knows nothing of a grid or a table.
- A run starts in Icons with nothing beyond registering: the runner writes
  `browser_mode = "icons"` into `settings.toml`, and `Registry::new` shows the view whose
  `shows` answers to it. Switching while the window is open is [A8](A8.md)'s.
- Not in the first pull request: context menus on the grid, thumbnails and the details
  line, position restore, the size keys and their saving, autoscroll. Until then a
  right-click on a tile only ends a rename.
