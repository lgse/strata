## Plan

1. **Portal chooser (`--portal` and `--qa --portal`), `Chooser` part, and image conversion** (`contract`):
   - Implement the XDG `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.FileChooser` v4 and `org.freedesktop.impl.portal.Request` D-Bus service (`org.freedesktop.impl.portal.desktop.strata`) in `crates/strata-app/src/portal.rs`, `--portal` in `launch.rs` (parented through `strata_platform::foreign_parent` with Hyprland placement/size hints), and `--qa --portal` (`windowless`, `toplevels`, `title`, `adopt`) in `crates/strata-app/src/qa/`.
   - Mount `Chooser` and `ChooserDialog` in `crates/strata-app/src/chooser/` with `ChooserFileSource` (glob/MIME filters, directory-only, `root_limit` containment, local-only), chooser window chrome & header, local-only sidebar & dialog-local toggle, single- vs multiple-selection rules, chooser file activation & `Name` entry, `Filter` & `Choice` controls, `"Open files read-only"`, Save overwrite confirmation, remote HTTP download reuse, and PNG image conversion consent/errors (`strata_preview::services::image_conversion`).
   - Forward blank-sidebar drags (`PlacesSidebar::on_background_drag` + 1 pt trailing resize border matching GTK's `sidebar-split > separator.wide`) into the shown view's marquee controller.
   - Flip 21 A20 runs to `required` (18 in `chooser_selection`, 3 in `chooser_image_conversion`) and re-point the 2 externally blocked `chooser_selection` runs to [A16](A16.md) (`chooser-pdf-zoom-keeps-text-size/list-default`) and [A19](A19.md) (`chooser-sidebar-drops-survive-resize/list`).
2. **Floating destination chooser (`Copy to…`, `Move to…`, `Extract to…`)** (`contract`):
   - Wire `copy-to`, `move-to`, and `extract-to` to open the floating folder chooser (`"Copy to"`, `"Move to"`, `"Extract to"`, `"Copy here"`, `"Move here"`, `"Extract here"`), feeding `transfer` and `archive::extract_to_chosen`, with secondary-toplevel QA support.
   - Flip the 5 cross-package floating-chooser runs (`dialogs_and_menus/floating-chooser-transfers-files/{columns-copy,columns-move}`, `dialogs_and_menus/compress-then-chooser-extracts/columns`, `archive_errors/abandoned-extract-to-no-hijack/{columns-cancelled-password,columns-read-only-destination}`), keeping their `package` fields unchanged.
3. **Mutations, fuzz alphabet, and closeout**:
   - Prove one Rune mutation patch per A20 scenario folder (`chooser_selection` and `chooser_image_conversion`).
   - Add `tests/qa/fuzz/alphabets/chooser.toml` and `pins`, pass the 20-seed differential smoke tier, and close the package.
