## Plan

The package's agent planned it this way at round 1 (2026-11-19), from F2's proposal and the
round's own timings.

### The long-run tier

- **What it is, per merged alphabet:** `./scripts/qa-fuzz.sh run --alphabet <area> --seeds
  1..1000 --target gtk,rune`, and `--length 120 --seeds 2001..2100`, two replays on each target:
  F2's proposal, adopted. Seeds 1 to 20 are the smoke tier's, so a round's first slice starts at
  21. `calibration` is the exception: type to search is on and it never agreed across the
  targets (F2's survey), so its long run is GTK against GTK (`--target gtk`), the noise
  calibration of `docs/testing.md`.
- **Taken in rounds, in slices.** A round runs a slice of 100 new seeds of each alphabet in its
  group (round 1: 21 to 120), and from round 2 ten of the 120-action seeds a round (2001 to
  2010, then 2011 to 2020). An alphabet's long run is done when 1 to 1000 and 2001 to 2100 have
  run on one version. A new version starts it again.
- **Budget:** at most about 20 minutes per command under `/tmp/strata-qa.lock`, so other
  agents' gates interleave. Round 1's rates, 100 seeds of 40 actions at 12 sequences at once:
  10 to 11.5 minutes for `icons`, `list`, `sidebar`, `clipboard`, `pointer`, `tenxer` and
  `drag`; 46 minutes for `settings` (run it in slices of 40 or fewer); 3.5 to 4 minutes per 25
  for `media` and `chooser`. A shrink is one command of its own: 3 to 15 minutes, 25 for one of
  89 replays.

### Rotation

Oldest and least compared first. Round 1: the three alphabets never compared across the
targets (`icons`, `list`, `sidebar`), then `clipboard`, `pointer`, `tenxer`, `drag`,
`settings`, `media` and `chooser`. Round 2: `columns`, `entries`, `location`, `views`,
`archives`, `filter`, `menus`, `open-with`, `preview`, `properties`, `tabs`, and `calibration`
on GTK. From round 3, each group's next slice in the same order, with an alphabet that changed
version going to the front. A new area alphabet joins the next round.

### Triage

- **Classes first.** A run's `DIFFER` lines are grouped by their first field and last action.
  Each class is searched in [`findings.md`](../findings.md) by field, action and area, then
  one seed of it is shrunk (`gtk,rune`; an invariant on Rune alone) and confirmed 3 of 3. The
  run's own GTK replays agreeing is the first check that it is no GTK race; 24 GTK replays when
  one is suspected.
- **Then the plan's [order of resort](../README.md#when-a-run-differs-the-order-of-resort).** A
  Rune bug that is small and in reach is fixed in the round's pull request with a test that
  fails without the fix, and its sequence kept in the corpus unparked when it agrees across the
  targets; a bigger one, or one in a `contract` seam, gets a finding and its sequence parked.
  A Strata fault is an `oracle` finding. A difference that is a choice goes to the owner in
  [`owner.md`](../owner.md). A class that is a known finding parks its shrunk sequence against
  that finding, and the entry gains a line naming the seeds.
- **Never widen** the map or the mask without a cited finding and, for the map, a ruling.
- **An alphabet merged before the differential tier** (`icons v1`, `list v3`, `sidebar v1`)
  differs in nearly every seed on classes already recorded or ruled pending. Its classes are
  recorded and only those not yet known are shrunk; its long run counts once it has a version
  held to the differential tier (guards for the known classes), which the round after proposes.

### Corpus upkeep

Each round replays every area's parked sequences on both targets (`replay --parked --alphabet
<area> --target gtk,rune`) and the unparked corpus (`replay --target gtk,rune`). A parked
sequence that agrees is unparked when its finding is fixed, by a round's fix or another
package's; one that agrees while its finding is open stays, as a race agrees most of the time
(#2180), unless the round shows why it agrees now. Every unparked sequence must agree.

### "Delegated in rounds until exit", for E2

F4 is done when, on one head after the last area alphabet merged: every merged alphabet has a
version held to the differential tier and has run its whole long-run tier on it; every
divergence the tier met is triaged, fixed or parked against a finding with its reference; and
the parked corpus has been surveyed on that head. Each round is one pull request with this
file's record. [E2](E2.md)'s fuzz soak then repeats the long-run commands on the exit head for
the hours the owner sets, and counts any divergence not parked as new. F4's exit is not E2's:
the plan's exit criterion 4 needs the soak to pass with no open `divergence` finding
([README](../README.md#exit-criteria)), so every finding F4 parks as a `divergence` (#2626,
#2627, #2632, #2551 and #2406's open cases, today) is fixed, or ruled into another code, before
E2 can pass; parking one is enough for F4, not for the exit.
