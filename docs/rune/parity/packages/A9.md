## What dependents must know

- **`Selection::clear` keeps the anchor, and a missing anchor stays missing through `sync`** (C1, `crates/strata-components/src/collection/selection/mod.rs`, finding [#2551](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2551)). After Escape or a plain press on empty space, a Shift+click ranges from the anchor the selection had, as GTK's `clear_active_selection` and `unselect_all` leave it. After a folder's load in List or Icons, the cursor has no anchor, so a Shift+click selects the clicked entry alone. The keyboard is unchanged: with nothing selected, a Shift+arrow first moves the anchor to the cursor. A view that wants the anchor on its cursor sets it with `AnchorRule::Move`.
- **The `pointer` alphabet sends no keyboard cursor moves** (finding [#2552](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2552), `oracle`, and [#2414](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2414)), no Settings (A18's), and no row drags (A19's). Those areas' alphabets cover them.
