## Survey

The coverage gaps H5 took up, against the feature map (`wmfeht/strata-feature-map`) and
`tests/qa/scenarios`, as of 2026-11-19.

Now covered (GTK scenarios in `h5-integration`):

| Area | Scenarios | Behaviour pinned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tab session restore (B2's note) | `tabs/restore-tabs-on-plain-launch-only`, `tabs/restore-tabs-drops-missing-folders`, `tabs/restore-tabs-setting-gates-session` | Restore on a plain launch only, in tab order with the saved active tab; a folder argument restores nothing and never writes `tabs.toml`; a missing folder's, a file's and a relative path's tabs close, and a lone missing tab opens the default folder; with Restore open tabs off nothing is restored and `tabs.toml` is deleted, turning it on resumes saving and turning it off deletes the file at once |
| The Menu key with the cursor's entry scrolled away (B3's note) | `keyboard_navigation/menu-key-skips-scrolled-away-cursor` (Columns and Icons only, so not finding [#2235](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2235)) | Menu and Shift+F10 open no menu and select nothing in view; with the entry back in view, Menu opens its menu |
| Devices and mounts (the plan's example) | `devices/device-rows-follow-plug-and-unplug`, `devices/device-mount-and-unmount`, `devices/device-eject-unplugs-and-leaves`, `devices/device-mount-failure-reports-error` | The DEVICES heading and rows follow plug and unplug, with the heading's item count; a click mounts and opens an unmounted volume; Unmount unmounts (not ejects) and leaves for Home; Eject ejects the drive, its row goes and the browser leaves; a failed mount shows "Unable to mount volume" with GIO's text |

Each has a mutation patch under `tests/qa/mutations/` that it is proven to catch, and a `quality`
header. Two harness pieces came with them: the runner's plain restart
(`{"restart":{"plain":true}}`) and a fake GVfs volume monitor (`"volume_monitor":true`), with
`hold_ms` on `fs` checks. `docs/testing.md` ("Fake devices") and the qa-scenarios reference
describe them on `main`'s side.

Still gaps:

- Devices: encrypted volumes (Unlock and Lock), password-start drives, network-share rows and
  Disconnect, Send to… device destinations, and the "Connecting…" wait on a mount another
  process owns. The fake monitor could serve each with a few more device fields.
- Tab restore: `trash:///` and `recent:///` tabs, the 32-tab cap, and a folder whose check
  times out on a stalled mount (which needs a hung-mount fake).
- `scripts/qa-drive.sh` cannot start the fake monitor, so a device session is driven only
  through scenarios.

## Blocker

CI publishes test images only for `main` and `strata-next` (`.github/workflows/test-image.yml`,
on a push or a `workflow_dispatch` there). `h5-integration` has #2619's image key, but no
published image for it. So a later pull request into `h5-integration` that does not touch
`tests/container` fails CI's "Resolve the shared environment" step ("The unchanged environment
is unavailable"); one that does builds an unpublished candidate.

Resolved by the owner's ruling of 2026-11-20: a test image built locally from the recipe
(`python3 scripts/image_base.py build`) stands in for the published one until one is published,
and validates pull requests into `h5-integration` too, where that CI failure is expected.

## Follow-up on `main`

From [A16](A16.md)'s review: finding [#2612](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2612) (`oracle`, in
[`findings.md`](../findings.md)). GTK names the audio player's album art and spectrum with a
bare `update_property(&[gtk::accessible::Property::Label(…)])`, in
`src/ui/preview/audio/artwork.rs:170` and `src/ui/preview/audio/spectrum.rs:114`, and GTK has no
public getter for an accessible label, so GTK's QA snapshot reads both unnamed. Naming them with
`crate::ui::accessibility::set_label` would let A16's fuzz-map `oracle` rule for the two names
go. It is a fix for `main`, through its own issue and pull request.

## Notes from other packages

From [B2](B2.md) (2026-11-12): **tab session restore has no QA scenario.** Nothing under
`tests/qa/scenarios` sets `restore_tabs` or seeds or reads `tabs.toml`; the behaviour is
tested only by GTK's unit tests in `src/ui/window/composition/tabs/tests.rs`, which go with
`src/ui` at parity. A scenario would pin: restore on a plain launch only, tab order and the
active tab, no restore with a folder argument, a missing folder's tab, and nothing restored
while `restore_tabs` is off. The rules, with where GTK has each, are in
`docs/rune/shared-state.md`, "Tab sessions". On the Rune target such a scenario also needs
the offscreen host to flush the settings writer at exit, which it does not do today.

From [B3](B3.md) (2026-11-12): **no scenario presses the Menu key or Shift+F10 with the
cursor's entry scrolled out of view, and one written from GTK's List view would encode a
fault.** There GTK opens the menu of whichever entry is drawn where the cursor's row was
last placed and selects it (finding [#2235](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2235) in [`findings.md`](../findings.md), seen in a run);
Columns and Icons open nothing. The Rune interface opens nothing in every view, on purpose.
A scenario for this gap takes Columns or Icons as its oracle, or waits for the fix on
`main`; `strata-app`'s unit tests cover the Rune side meanwhile
(`commands/tests/menu_key.rs`).
