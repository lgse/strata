## Notes from other packages

From [B2](B2.md) (2026-11-12): **tab session restore has no QA scenario.** Nothing under
`tests/qa/scenarios` sets `restore_tabs` or seeds or reads `tabs.toml`; the behaviour is
tested only by GTK's unit tests in `src/ui/window/composition/tabs/tests.rs`, which go with
`src/ui` at parity. A scenario would pin: restore on a plain launch only, tab order and the
active tab, no restore with a folder argument, a missing folder's tab, and nothing restored
while `restore_tabs` is off. The rules, with where GTK has each, are in
`docs/rune/shared-state.md`, "Tab sessions". On the Rune target such a scenario also needs
the offscreen host to flush the settings writer at exit, which it does not do today.
