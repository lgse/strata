## Why it is held

H5 writes its scenarios on `main` first, and they reach `strata-next` by merge. Agents were
authorized to merge their own pull requests into `strata-next`, not into `main`, so the
orchestrating session holds the package for the owner (recorded by the orchestrating
session on 2026-11-12; [`owner.md`](../owner.md#h5-coverage-gap-scenarios)).

## Notes from other packages

From [B2](B2.md) (2026-11-12): **tab session restore has no QA scenario.** Nothing under
`tests/qa/scenarios` sets `restore_tabs` or seeds or reads `tabs.toml`; the behaviour is
tested only by GTK's unit tests in `src/ui/window/composition/tabs/tests.rs`, which go with
`src/ui` at parity. A scenario would pin: restore on a plain launch only, tab order and the
active tab, no restore with a folder argument, a missing folder's tab, and nothing restored
while `restore_tabs` is off. The rules, with where GTK has each, are in
`docs/rune/shared-state.md`, "Tab sessions". On the Rune target such a scenario also needs
the offscreen host to flush the settings writer at exit, which it does not do today.

From [B3](B3.md) (2026-11-12): **no scenario presses the Menu key or Shift+F10 with the
cursor's entry scrolled out of view, and one written from GTK's List view would encode a
fault.** There GTK opens the menu of whichever entry is drawn where the cursor's row was
last placed and selects it (finding [#2235](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2235) in [`findings.md`](../findings.md), seen in a run);
Columns and Icons open nothing. The Rune interface opens nothing in every view, on purpose.
A scenario for this gap takes Columns or Icons as its oracle, or waits for the fix on
`main`; `strata-app`'s unit tests cover the Rune side meanwhile
(`commands/tests/menu_key.rs`).
