## Notes from other packages

From [A21](A21.md) (2026-11-19, [#2620](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2620)): **the fuzz
driver cannot drive windows**, and the owner ruled on 2026-11-19 that a driver that can is later
F or H work. It replays with no session bus (`bus: None` in `tests/qa/fuzz/replay.rs`), so a
`launch` step has no primary to reach; the QA protocol has no step that moves the keyboard to
another window (the Rune QA host keeps keys on the run's window, where GTK's new window takes
the X focus); and the observations are the run's window's (`sample.app`). With a bus and a
window-switch step, an alphabet could launch windows, open folders in new ones (Shift+Enter,
`open-in-window`) and act across them; A21 closed with its runs and window tests standing in
([`owner.md`](../owner.md#ruled)).
