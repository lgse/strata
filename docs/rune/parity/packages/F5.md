## Plan

The package's agent planned it this way on 2026-11-19, after reading H4, F2, F4, A19, A20 and
A21. What the offscreen host cannot reach, and so what needs the session host
(`docs/testing.md`, "The session host"):

| Reach | Needs from the driver | Slice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pointer and row drags through a compositor: every press, motion and release through Wayland's virtual pointer, and drags through the compositor's data device | Rune replays in a private headless sway, per replay | 1 |
| Launches with arguments, FileManager1's calls, a second window, a switch between windows, closing one: the windows [A21](A21.md) could not fuzz, since its session host serves no launch | A private session bus per replay, as a `session_bus` scenario has; the `launch` and `dbus_send` runner steps replayed; a window-switch step both targets take; the windows beyond the run's own compared, where A20's projection keeps them only as `context` | 2 |
| Drags and drops between processes, and the system clipboard with another client: [A19](A19.md)'s content drops and A3's foreign folder drags | The `foreign` peer steps replayed; on GTK the X11 peer as `content_drops` runs it today | 3 |
| The portal chooser in a real window | `--qa --portal` on the session host, which [A20](A20.md) merged with #2598 (`crates/strata-app/src/qa/session/portal.rs`); the header's `portal` allowed in a sequence | 4 |

1. **Slice 1, the driver and `session-pointer`** (`contract`: the runner in `tests/qa`).
   - **The session host as a fuzz host.** An alphabet names its host (`host = "session"`),
     and `--host offscreen|session` overrides it for a survey. A Rune replay on the session host
     gets a private headless sway of its own, as a `host = "session"` row does in
     `qa-parity.sh`, and `strata-rune --qa` serves from a real window there; its GTK replays stay
     on Xvfb, the reference as in every parity run. Toolkit-neutral: the steps are the same
     scenario steps on both targets, and only the display differs. A stream's header says the
     display it ran on, a summary says which areas' Rune replays ran there, and their artifacts
     go under `<targets>-session`. The host is pinned when it is `session`, so moving an alphabet
     between hosts raises its version. No bus, no window-switch step and no wider observation
     yet: slice 1's alphabet needs none.
   - **`session-pointer`** (v3 as merged): what [A9](A9.md)'s `pointer` and [A19](A19.md)'s
     `drag` reach in List, together, through the compositor: clicks, Ctrl and Shift clicks, slow
     second clicks, background clicks, marquees, the context menu by the pointer and dismissed,
     files dragged and Ctrl-dragged onto folders, folders onto folders,
     drops on the pane's background, undo, Back and the parent, and a file changed behind the
     application's back. It leaves out what those two leave out, and Escape or a modifier
     change during a drag, which wlroots' drag grab never delivers (A19, "Open").
   - The tier: `check`, a 20-seed GTK calibration, a 20-seed differential smoke tier, until
     clean, each divergence triaged.
2. **Slice 2, windows and launches**, the driver [A21](A21.md)'s note below asks for and the
   owner ruled on 2026-11-19 later F or H work, so the coordinator's to place
   (`contract`: the runner, and the session host in
   `crates/strata-app/src/qa/session.rs`). The session host's QA mode claims the runner's bus
   and serves a launch's window, the item A21 deferred ("Open"); the driver starts a private bus
   for an alphabet whose header asks for one and replays `launch` and `dbus_send`. A
   window-switch step for both targets, on sway by its IPC and on Xvfb by the GTK harness's
   own activation, is new protocol: a `contract` to `strata-qa`, with its GTK half on `main`
   (below). The windows open beyond the run's own are already in each observation, as A20's
   `context.toplevels` and `context.window_focused` (`tests/qa/fuzz/project.rs`), which only a
   rule's `while` reads: slice 2 decides which of them to compare, rather than adding a key. Alphabet `session-windows`: launches of a folder, a file
   and nothing, FileManager1's `ShowFolders` and `ShowItems`, a switch, a close, and the
   pointer and keys in whichever window has them.
3. **Slice 3, drags between processes** (`contract`: the runner). The foreign drag source and
   the clipboard peer as replayed steps, and `session-drops`: text, images, files and links
   from another client onto a view, a tab, a crumb and a sidebar row, and a copy pasted from
   another client. Taken first of slices 3 and 4, on the coordinator's word of 2026-11-19,
   since slice 2's GTK half waits on `main`: neither needs a GTK harness change, the runner's
   steps being the ones `content_drops` runs on both targets. Its first pull request ([#2656](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2656))
   replays the foreign drag source and a header's session bus, with `session-drops v2`'s text
   drops; files, links, the image server, the clipboard peer and the other drop targets are
   its next.
4. **Slice 4, the portal**, on A20's portal session host (`qa/session/portal.rs`, #2598):
   `session-portal`, the chooser's pointer selection and drops, beside A20's offscreen
   `chooser` alphabet.

**Seeds and budget.** Each alphabet's smoke tier is F2's: 20 seeds of 40 actions, two replays
on each target, `--target gtk,rune`. A Rune replay on the session host costs a sway and a
lavapipe window; slice 1 measured `session-pointer`'s tier at 2 min 44 s at the default 12 at
once at v2, 2 min 8 s at v3 (`docs/testing.md`, "Tiers and budgets"), so a session alphabet takes the default `--jobs`.
The long run is [F4](F4.md)'s rotation: at that rate F2's proposal, 1,000 seeds of 40 actions,
takes a session alphabet about two hours.

**Triage.** The plan's order of resort, as for any alphabet. A divergence the session host shows
and the offscreen host does not (`--host offscreen` on the same seed) is in the windowed path:
the windowed host, Rune's input through winit, or the session QA backend; the last is
`harness` and is fixed in `qa/session.rs`. A compositor limit (no key during a drag, the US
layout) is guarded in the alphabet, never normalised: the plan's `platform` code allows no rule.
Each class is replayed 24 times on GTK alone before Rune is blamed for it, shrunk GTK against
Rune and parked as `docs/testing.md` says.

**How it feeds E2.** [E2](E2.md)'s fuzz soak runs every merged alphabet, so each session
alphabet joins it with its host taken from its file: E2 needs the session alphabets' commands
and their cost from this package's file, and no open `divergence` finding of theirs.

**What `main` would need.** Slice 1 needs nothing of GTK: its replays run on Xvfb as before.
Slice 2's window-switch step, and a snapshot key for the windows if it takes one, need GTK's QA
mode on `main` to answer them (`src/qa`); that work goes to the `h5-integration` branch by the
owner's ruling, routed by the coordinator, and slice 2 waits for it.

## What dependents must know

- **A session alphabet** says `host = "session"`; its smoke tier is the same command as any
  (`docs/testing.md`, "On the session host"), and its Rune artifacts are under
  `target/qa-fuzz/<area>/gtk-rune-session/`. `--host session` surveys any alphabet in a real
  window. GTK never leaves Xvfb.
- **A command's GLib work now runs at once on a real window.** A handler outside
  `commands::call` that attaches GLib work (a `spawn_local`, an idle) and draws nothing still
  waits for the next frame: wake the context after it, as `commands::wake_glib` does.
- **E2**: `./scripts/qa-fuzz.sh run --alphabet session-pointer --seeds 1..20 --target gtk,rune`
  and `--alphabet session-drops` are the session host's smoke tiers, about 2 to 3 and 4
  minutes.
- **A sequence may start a drag source in another process** and take a session bus
  (`docs/testing.md`, "Drops from another process"). An alphabet of drops compares no focus
  after its first drop, under #2595's rules.

## Notes from other packages

From [A21](A21.md) (2026-11-19, [#2620](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2620)): **the fuzz
driver cannot drive windows**, and the owner ruled on 2026-11-19 that a driver that can is later
F or H work. It replays with no session bus (`bus: None` in `tests/qa/fuzz/replay.rs`), so a
`launch` step has no primary to reach; the QA protocol has no step that moves the keyboard to
another window (the Rune QA host keeps keys on the run's window, where GTK's new window takes
the X focus); and the observations are the run's window's (`sample.app`). With a bus and a
window-switch step, an alphabet could launch windows, open folders in new ones (Shift+Enter,
`open-in-window`) and act across them; A21 closed with its runs and window tests standing in
([`owner.md`](../owner.md#ruled)).
