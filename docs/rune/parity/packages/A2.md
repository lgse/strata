## Notes from other packages

From [B1](B1.md), on what a view is in `crates/strata-app/src/slots.rs` (2026-11-12). Nothing
here is this package's to change in passing: a file another package owns changes in a
`contract` pull request of its own.

- **What a view writes:** a module with its state, `impl Part` for it, and its two lines in
  `lib.rs` under its package's comment. Its place is `Place::View(View { build, shows,
  focus, pointer_at, rows, editing, probe })`. `columns/part.rs` is the worked example and
  `test_support::idle_view` a view whose every call does nothing.
- **`shows`** says whether the view is the one the saved `browser_mode` names. The GTK
  application also reads `explorer` as List, and that reading has not moved, so the List
  view's `shows` answers to both `list` and `explorer`.
- **The listing is shared:** `listing.rs` and `events.rs` belong to the contract. A view's
  listener calls `events::read` and applies each `Change` to its own component's state.
- **`columns/`** has no area package. If a second view needs part of it, [A1](A1.md) opens
  that `contract` change and A2 waits for it.
- **`rows`** is all the context menu asks of a view; what a chosen row does is decided once,
  in `menus::chosen`. **`editing`** is what the rename editor asks; a view without a
  follower gives `None` for `hold` and `follow_now`.
- **The keyboard:** `focus` is the key that takes it back from a field or a menu; where the
  keyboard leaves the view at its start, the view calls `shell::leave_view_at_start`.
- **`probe`** tells the QA backend how to read the view's panes. If they do not fit, or
  `qa/roles.rs` has to learn a role, that is a `contract` change with [H3](H3.md).
- A view is chosen once, from the saved mode; a live switch is [A8](A8.md)'s.
  `test_support::hosted_over` tests a place without registering it.
