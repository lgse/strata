## What dependents must know

- **`Viewer::Pdf`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/pdf.rs`) holds C12's `PdfState`, keyed
  `preview-pdf`. Its labels are `preview::pdf::labels()`. A password-protected PDF keeps the
  drawer's message card with its Open button (`Viewer::Message`, from `load::render_failed`).
- **The PDF's zoom reset is the key map's `pdf_zoom_reset`**, the command `preview-pdf-reset`
  at `Stage::ZoomShortcut` while the keyboard is in the pages (`preview::pdf::RESET`). The
  preview's commands are asked before the window's own `text-reset`, as GTK's `zoom_shortcut`
  asks `reset_pdf_zoom` first. C12's own Ctrl+0 is off (`PdfView::reset_key(|_| false)`), so a
  rebound reset follows the key map and an unbound Ctrl+0 reaches the text size.
  `PdfState::reset_zoom` is public for it.
- **The window's zoom wheel** (`crates/strata-app/src/appearance/wheel.rs`) is GTK's
  `zoom_controller`. Ctrl+wheel steps the text size (`text_size::step`). Ctrl+Shift+wheel steps
  the Icons tiles while Icons shows (`icons::size::step`), and over the grid it is C3's
  `WheelSteps`. With Alt or Super the wheel scrolls. The PDF's pages keep Ctrl+wheel for their
  own zoom. Rune has no wheel capture (packet entry 10, case 1), so `wheel::heard` is asked from
  the window's root element, each Columns column (`ColumnsView::collection`), the List rows and
  a filter's results (`single::wheeled`), and the Icons grids (`wheel::over_grid`).
  - **A part that adds a scroll view** with a wheel hook should ask `wheel::heard` from it.
  - **Not covered:** over the sidebar, the preview's text views and a dialog's page, which have
    no hook, the wheel scrolls.
- **The QA backend's node paths** leave a PDF row's zoom generation out (`qa::tree::segment_of`),
  so a page remembered before a zoom is the same page after it, as GTK's widget is.
- **`Viewer::Model`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/model.rs`, keyed `preview-model`)
  - **The frame:** a model file's load shows the frame at once (`model::opening`, from
    `load::start_request`), and the provider's `Progress` stages fill its spinner.
  - **A camera button** asks the provider again with that `model_view`, as GTK's
    `change_model_view` does.
  - **The palette** comes from the look: the glow's colour for GTK's accent, `surface_raised`
    for its surface.
  - **A first load that fails** is the drawer's message card.
- **`Viewer::Route`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/route.rs`, keyed `preview-route`)
  - **Showing:** it shows once its PNG images are decoded; `Viewer::Decoding` stands in meanwhile.
  - **The media keys** are the preview's shared ones (`preview::transport`, below): a route
    takes play, pause and the seeks.
  - **Units:** each poll gives it the saved `unit_system`.
- **`Viewer::Media`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/media/`, keyed `preview-media`): C13's
  player for `PreviewContent::SandboxedMedia`, by type video, audio or the plain bar (a GIF,
  which loops).
  - **Silent playback** (`media/stream.rs`): the shared `sandbox::media::Session`, drained on
    GLib's context into Rune through `Later`, three frames ahead at most, played on the poll's
    clock. Packet entry 26 retires it; until then every player is silent and no shim is
    imported.
  - **Stepping** (`media/sequence.rs`): which files of the kind count (the probe found media,
    or the leading bytes, read through the mount lane, back the name), and the step, through
    the shown view's `rows.select_only` and `rows.reveal`. The answers live on the preview
    (`Preview::media_checks`), so a step reads only what it has not read.
  - **Tests:** `preview::media::START_DECODER` puts a fixture stream
    (`strata_preview::sandbox::media::fixtures`, a dev-dependency feature) in the sandbox's
    place, and `Recorded::answer_as` answers with a content type.
- **The preview's media keys** (`preview::transport`): `preview-media-play-pause`,
  `-seek-back`, `-seek-forward`, `-volume-up`, `-volume-down`, `-mute`, `-previous` and `-next`,
  one per catalog command at `Stage::VideoControls`, where `preview::keys::media_keys_reach`
  holds (an open drawer; the keyboard in no text field, the sidebar or the header, which is
  `Situation::in_top_bar`) and a player or a route shows. A new player kind adds its arm to
  `transport::run`.
- **The volume slider is named "Volume"**, where GTK's is unnamed: a ruled `deviation`
  (finding [#2606](https://github.com/lgse/strata/issues/2606), [`owner.md`](../owner.md#ruled),
  2026-11-18). A run or a fuzz step that reads it, Slice 4's alphabet included, cites the
  ruling.
- **C13's `Labels`** (`contract`): one tooltip per step button
  (`previous_video_tooltip` and its siblings, each a whole message) in place of
  `step_tooltip`, and `position` for the eyebrow. `Labels` is no longer `PartialEq`.
- **The eyebrow** ("2 of 4 in folder") is the host's: `ModelLabels::position` and
  `RouteLabels::position` (additive, defaulting to English) take
  `preview::labels::position_caption`, and `labels::listing_position` counts the previewed
  column's files of a kind, in its order, hidden ones left out while hidden. Search results are
  not yet counted as GTK's `in results`.
- **A viewer's accessors never panic.** Rune can dispatch a handler it queued after the viewer
  changed in the same frame, so an accessor for a viewer that has gone returns
  `viewer::Detached`'s spare state, which nothing reads, and logs it at `debug`
  (`viewer::spare`). Every viewer's accessors do, A15's included (`viewer::shown` and
  `shown_mut`); a new viewer's add their spare state to `Detached`.
- **Pictures and players** are dispatched from the provider's answer by `preview::pictures`
  (an image, a model, a route, media, a PDF page); text and documents stay in `load`.
- **The `media` fuzz alphabet** (`tests/qa/fuzz/alphabets/media.toml`) holds the model, the route
  and two videos, and leaves PDFs, audio and GIFs out until #2560's PDF facet is ruled and
  #2612 and #2613 are settled; its comment says how to bring each back. A step that leaves a
  player playing at a step boundary reads a moving time, so it plays only from a video's end.
- **A map rule may name what Rune's list adds** (`{"rune_adds":[…]}`, `tests/qa/fuzz/rules.rs`):
  `app.preview`'s "Volume" (#2606) is its first. A name another ruling lets Rune add takes a
  rule of its own.
- **A media step carries the keyboard** (`media::Stepped`): a step from a player that had the
  keyboard gives the next player it once it mounts.
- **The test provider** (`preview/tests/provider.rs`): `Recorded` stands in for the sandboxed
  provider (`Loader::set_provider`, tests only), keeps every request, and answers, fails or
  reports a stage when told.
