## What dependents must know

- **`Viewer::Pdf`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/pdf.rs`) holds C12's `PdfState`, keyed
  `preview-pdf`. Its labels are `preview::pdf::labels()`. A password-protected PDF keeps the
  drawer's message card with its Open button (`Viewer::Message`, from `load::render_failed`).
- **The PDF's zoom reset is the key map's `pdf_zoom_reset`**, the command `preview-pdf-reset`
  at `Stage::ZoomShortcut` while the keyboard is in the pages (`preview::pdf::RESET`). The
  preview's commands are asked before the window's own `text-reset`, as GTK's `zoom_shortcut`
  asks `reset_pdf_zoom` first. C12's own Ctrl+0 is off (`PdfView::reset_key(|_| false)`), so a
  rebound reset follows the key map and an unbound Ctrl+0 reaches the text size.
  `PdfState::reset_zoom` is public for it.
- **The window's zoom wheel** (`crates/strata-app/src/appearance/wheel.rs`) is GTK's
  `zoom_controller`. Ctrl+wheel steps the text size (`text_size::step`). Ctrl+Shift+wheel steps
  the Icons tiles while Icons shows (`icons::size::step`), and over the grid it is C3's
  `WheelSteps`. With Alt or Super the wheel scrolls. The PDF's pages keep Ctrl+wheel for their
  own zoom. Rune has no wheel capture (packet entry 10, case 1), so `wheel::heard` is asked from
  the window's root element, each Columns column (`ColumnsView::collection`), the List rows and
  a filter's results (`single::wheeled`), and the Icons grids (`wheel::over_grid`).
  - **A part that adds a scroll view** with a wheel hook should ask `wheel::heard` from it.
  - **Not covered:** over the sidebar, the preview's text views and a dialog's page, which have
    no hook, the wheel scrolls.
- **The QA backend's node paths** leave a PDF row's zoom generation out (`qa::tree::segment_of`),
  so a page remembered before a zoom is the same page after it, as GTK's widget is.
- **`Viewer::Model`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/model.rs`, keyed `preview-model`)
  - **The frame:** a model file's load shows the frame at once (`model::opening`, from
    `load::start_request`), and the provider's `Progress` stages fill its spinner.
  - **A camera button** asks the provider again with that `model_view`, as GTK's
    `change_model_view` does.
  - **The palette** comes from the look: the glow's colour for GTK's accent, `surface_raised`
    for its surface.
  - **A first load that fails** is the drawer's message card.
- **`Viewer::Route`** (`crates/strata-app/src/preview/route.rs`, keyed `preview-route`)
  - **Showing:** it shows once its PNG images are decoded; `Viewer::Decoding` stands in meanwhile.
  - **The media keys:** `preview-route-play-pause`, `preview-route-seek-back` and
    `preview-route-seek-forward`, at `Stage::VideoControls`, play, pause and seek it (GTK's `handle_video_key`) outside the sidebar and text fields. This
    package's slice 3 widens it to audio and video.
  - **Units:** each poll gives it the saved `unit_system`.
- **The eyebrow** ("2 of 4 in folder") is the host's: `ModelLabels::position` and
  `RouteLabels::position` (additive, defaulting to English) take
  `preview::labels::position_caption`, and `labels::listing_position` counts the previewed
  column's files of a kind, in its order, hidden ones left out while hidden. Search results are
  not yet counted as GTK's `in results`.
- **The test provider** (`preview/tests/provider.rs`): `Recorded` stands in for the sandboxed
  provider (`Loader::set_provider`, tests only), keeps every request, and answers, fails or
  reports a stage when told.
