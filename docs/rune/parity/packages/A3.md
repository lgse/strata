## What dependents must know

- **The sidebar's keys start with `sidebar`:** the list `sidebar-list`, a row
  `sidebar-place-<id>`, `sidebar-pin-<n>` or `sidebar-device-<id>`, the Trash's icon
  `sidebar-trash-icon`. `Situation::within("sidebar")` is true inside it. A pin's id is its
  line in the bookmarks file, counted from 0.
- **`shell::leave_view_at_start` lands on the sidebar's first row,** where GTK focuses the
  open place's row (`focus_active_place`): `Sidebar::focus` is one static key.
- **The sidebar acts on no drop and opens no menu yet:** its `PinHover`, `PinDrop`,
  `DropInto`, `DropHover` and `ContextMenu` intents are heard and dropped.

## Open

- **A known difference of the QA presentation, by the coordinator's ruling:** GTK exposes
  each pin gap to assistive technology as an empty group named "PINNED"; Rune keeps them out
  of the tree, as C8 decided, and only the QA backend will present them (plan item 2).
- **The sidebar's arrows out of it** (GTK's `sidebar_navigation`: Right back to the files, Up
  to the header) and **its Menu key** are B3's FocusNavigation, deferred to this package;
  only 10xer `h`, Left and Backspace leave it today.
- **Not yet ported:** the width toggle and its saved step, reordering, the sidebar's menus,
  devices, the full Trash's icon, the `g` keycaps, middle-click into a new tab (no tabs on
  this interface), the automatic rail, the update notice, and the sidebar's saved width.
- **C8's headings and list name are English** (`Section::title`, "Places"), where GTK's are
  translated; the component carries user-facing text there. For a later `contract` change.
- **Recent's second condition:** GTK also asks its `gtk-recent-files-enabled` setting, which
  this interface does not read, so it counts as on, its default.

## Notes from other packages

From [B1](B1.md) (2026-11-12):

- The sidebar's place is `Place::Sidebar(Sidebar { build, focus })`. A view whose keyboard
  leaves it at its start calls `shell::leave_view_at_start(app, tx)`, and the shell focuses
  the sidebar's `focus` key; the way back is `tx.focus(key, …)` with `shell::view_focus(app)`.
  `shell/tests.rs` has both directions with a made-up sidebar. A3 edits neither the Columns
  module nor the shell for this.
- One line of `slots.rs` is A3's to delete: the `expect(dead_code)` line on
  `Place::Sidebar`. Registering the sidebar makes clippy fail until that line, and only that
  line, is gone.
- Volumes and places come from services other than `Browser`: take `Setup::later` in
  `Part::new`, keep it in the sidebar's state, and queue with `later.then(…)` from a
  service's callback. A worker thread's result comes back through GLib and queues from
  there. Do not drain a queue of the sidebar's own in `Part::poll`.
- The header's Toggle sidebar button and the sidebar's key wait on [B3](B3.md).

From [B2](B2.md) (2026-11-12):

- Saving the sidebar's mode no longer waits: a part changes a setting with
  `shell::saved(app).change(|settings| …)` (merged in [#2178](https://github.com/lgse/strata/pull/2178)). The mode is a plain field of
  `Preferences`, so no setter moves.
- A guard GTK has and the Rune side lacks, which cannot fire today:
  `PreferenceManager::set_sort_preferences` stores nothing when handed a recency sort, and
  `Saved::store_view` has no such guard. Neither interface is ever handed one, because
  `Browser::record_sort` returns early at the Recent root. If that changes, the guard is one
  line in `store_view`, with a test that can fail.

From [H1](H1.md): `letter_bookmarks` stays with A3 although its three runs need 10xer mode,
a default for the owner to confirm ([`owner.md`](../owner.md#h1-whole-corpus-ledger)).
